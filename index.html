DISCIPLANT

Tend your habits. Watch them bloom.

Every habit is a seed. DISCIPLANT turns your daily practice into a living garden — six areas of life, six plant species, each one growing alongside the discipline you bring to it.

Add tasks in the categories that matter: study, move your body, find stillness, sleep well, keep your space and finances in order. Complete them each day and watch your plants grow. Miss a day and the chain breaks — so don't break it.

The garden reflects exactly what you tend. An oak that's weathered thirty days of consistent learning looks different from one that was just planted. That difference is visible, and it compounds.

Small actions, done daily, become something real. Start now — add your first task, check it off, and plant your first day.
